Only the owner decides the mission and the goals. An agent proposes a change, and it takes effect when the owner merges it.

## Goals

The states the owner most wants true now. They are priorities, not the whole of the work: fixing what broke and upkeep need no goal.

1. An Epic the owner starts runs to its end and removes the problem it came from, while the only waits that reach the owner are a question to answer and a pull request to merge.
2. Everything that waits on the owner reaches the owner: no question, hold, or unreviewed commit is found later by chance.
3. Nothing is built beyond what the owner decided: the owner finds no requirement in a change that the owner did not ask for.
